# 👩‍💼 Employee Productivity & HR Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes HR and workforce data to generate insights on **employee productivity, job satisfaction, and attrition trends**. An interactive **Tableau dashboard** was developed to enable HR teams and management to make **data-driven decisions** for workforce planning and employee retention.

The analysis uses the **IBM HR Analytics dataset** and explores employee demographics, departments, education, business travel, and satisfaction levels.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

* Assess employee distribution across departments and job roles
* Analyze job satisfaction by education field
* Examine demographic patterns such as gender and marital status
* Identify factors influencing employee engagement and attrition
* Support HR decision-making for workforce planning and retention

---

## 📂 Dataset

* **Dataset:** IBM HR Analytics – Employee Attrition Dataset
* **Source:** Kaggle ([Link](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/pavansubhasht/ibm-hr-analytics-attrition-dataset))

**Key Features Used:** Employee Number, Age, Gender, Marital Status, Department, Job Role, Education, Education Field, Business Travel, Job Satisfaction, Attrition

---

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies

* **Tableau Desktop** – Dashboard creation and visualization
* **CSV Dataset** – HR employee data
* **GitHub** – Version control and project documentation

---

## 📊 Key Metrics & KPIs

* Total and active employee count
* Employee distribution by department and gender
* Education level breakdown
* Average job satisfaction score
* Business travel frequency

---

## 📈 Dashboard Features

* Department-wise employee analysis
* Gender distribution visualization (pie chart)
* Education field breakdown (treemap)
* Average job satisfaction by education field
* Interactive filters for marital status and demographics
* User-friendly and interactive dashboard design

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

* R&D has the highest employee count
* Most employees hold Bachelor’s or Master’s degrees
* Employees from Life Sciences and Medical fields report higher job satisfaction
* Majority of employees rarely travel for business
* Male employees represent a larger share of the workforce
